TYREinflate 6000 (OTIR6000)

Date: 16.07.2025

 

Product Safety Recall

Limited Batch Recall Notice

PRODUCT: OSRAM TYREinflate 6000 (OTIR6000) - EAN 10: 4062172294515 | EAN 40: 4062172294522

BATCH NR.: The recall only affects the listed batch numbers: 35/21 | 36/21 | 09/22 | 10/22 | 11/22 | 12/22 | 15/22 | 16/22 | 24/22 | 29/22 | 02/23 | 03/23 | 04/23
The batch number is applied on the backside of the product (top label).
Due to a product change made from May 2023 onwards, all products made after May 2023 are not affected and safe to use. No other products or tyre inflators are being recalled.

WHY IS THIS PRODUCT DANGEROUS? Our investigations, conducted in partnership with our manufacturing and quality assurance teams, have identified a potential issue affecting certain batches of these products. Specifically, a fault in the lithium-ion battery protection loop may allow the battery to become overcharged, posing a potential risk of overheating or malfunction.

WHAT TO DO? FOR SAFETY REASONS, STOP USING THESE PRODUCTS & DISPOSE OF THEM PROPERLY. Please also inform your friends and family about this product recall. To request a replacement free of charge, please fill out the online form.

FURTHER INFORMATION: We take the quality and safety of our products extremely seriously and would like to apologize to customers affected for any inconvenience this may cause. For further information please contact our support service: automotive-service@osram.com. If you prefer having your current device repaired instead of receiving a replacement, please contact automotive-service@osram.com

We kindly request that you primarily use our email address automotive-service@osram.com for any communication related to the product recall. This will help us respond to your inquiries promptly and efficiently. However, if you need to speak with us over the phone, you may call our product recall hotline at +49 152 0100 8721. Please note that our hotline is only available during business hours, and our representatives can assist you in English or German.
